Final Mission Patch

This mission patch is an artistic representation of Baruch College in Space. In this patch, I chose three main symbols to create a triangular visual composition: A large blue “B” that symbolizes Baruch College, planet Earth, and a large star. I chose to connect these symbols because I wanted to illustrate Baruch’s immense passion for teaching and guiding their students towards becoming professionals in the corporate world, hence, reaching the stars in their accomplishments. In my opinion, it is a very respectable decision to help nurture young adults in discovering their interests and goals in life - especially considering that Baruch is a public university. Many students could get college degrees with little to no debt due to their program(s). This design reflects Baruch’s participation in scientific research and discovery in space through the visualisation of “space” and the star(s). Despite Baruch’s reputation for its business majors, many professors are well versed in their respective fields of scientific studies - some even recognized as those among the “World’s Top 2% Scientists” in 2024. The strive to keep on learning and teaching brings the brightest lights out of the people in this community.

A.I. Disclosure

AI tools, namely Gemini Pro and Chat GPT, were used during the development of this design. The purpose of these tools was to create a mission patch generator, using our hand drawn designs, based on a previous assignment used to construct stylized posters. The AI tool was asked to take our hand drawn sketches and the poster generator to create a similar generator in order to design the sketch using vector lines and shapes. The (finalized) result is as presented.
